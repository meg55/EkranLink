EkranLink turns your Android tablet or phone into a monitor for your Windows PC, over Wi-Fi or a USB cable.

<b>How it works</b>
1. Install EkranLink for Windows on your PC. It is free and open source: https://github.com/meg55/EkranLink/releases
2. Open EkranLink on your tablet or phone and join the same Wi-Fi network as the PC.
3. Click Connect on the PC and confirm the same 6-digit code on both screens. From then on it connects by itself whenever EkranLink is open.

<b>Features</b>
• Wi-Fi needs no setup on the device. A USB cable works too (turn on USB debugging once).
• Touch works like a Windows touch screen: scroll, pinch to zoom, press and hold for right click, on-screen keyboard. A simple mouse mode is also available.
• With Auto-rotate on, turn the device upright and the Windows screen turns portrait.
• Several tablets and phones at once, each with its own settings.
• Low latency and easy on the battery: the picture is decoded in hardware, and nothing is sent while the screen is still.
• Optionally shows the Windows sign-in screen too (service mode on the PC).
• English and Turkish.

<b>Privacy and security</b>
• No account, no ads, no tracking. The app talks only to the PCs you connect it to, over your own network or cable.
• Connections are encrypted (ECDH P-256 and ChaCha20-Poly1305).
• Once paired, the device answers only its own PCs, so others on the same Wi-Fi do not see it.

<b>Good to know</b>
• Needs EkranLink for Windows (Windows 10 or 11), a separate free download from the link above.
• Extend (the device as an extra monitor) and Only this need EkranLink's virtual display driver. Until the driver ships with the Windows installer, EkranLink mirrors the PC screen.
• Open source (GPL-3.0): https://github.com/meg55/EkranLink
